EmPath, short for Employment Pathway, is a cross-platform desktop program for keeping track of Software Engineers' job applications. We don't guarantee that you will become a Transcend, but we can at least help you on your Employment Pathway to become a gainfully-employed and productive CRUD monkey for a startup or maybe the next Morgan Polysoft. The program calculates weekly job search KPIs which are displayed in the Dashboard. Applications, Documents, and Contacts are viewed in their correspoinding windows. Some actions are also performed on the Admin Panel.

  • Job Offers
  • Take Home Assignments Received / Completed
  • Job Interviews Scheduled / Held
  • Follow Up Actions Taken
  • Applications Submitted
  • Cover Letters Written
  • Informational Interviews Scheduled / Held
  • New Contacts
  • Coding Challenge Goal Met
  • etc.

Applications are shown here with the following associated data:

  • Position Title
  • Employer
  • Location(s)
  • Requistion Number
  • Link to Original Job Posting
  • Description
  • Minimum Payment
  • Maximum Payment
  • Payment Period
  • Date Sourced
  • Date Applied
  • Status
  • Resume
  • Additional Notes
  • Resumes
  • Cover Letters
  • Snippets for copy-pasting into online forms

Rate contacts based on their helpfulness in your job search. All start out by deafault as Workers, but if they are insightful in an informational interview, you can set their status to Engineer, or if they are taking serious efforts to help you land the position, you can promote them to an Empath. Not so helpful? Then label them a Drone. If they try to scam you, then Nerve Staple them. You may be a Drone yourself right now, but don't let that stop you from engaging in a little catharsis as you dream of becoming the next faction leader.

EmPath is built with Python, Django, and FlaskWebGUI. As such, the user has access to Django's built-in Admin Panel. Some CRUD operations may be performed here; others may be performed on the appropriate window.
